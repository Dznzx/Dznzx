### 🚀 Currently building

**[Academic Twin](https://github.com/Dznzx/academic-twin-2)** — an AI study companion that reads *your* course material.

Upload your PDFs and notes, then chat with them, generate quizzes and flashcards, and get a study plan built around your weak topics. Retrieval-augmented, so every answer cites the source it came from.

`Next.js 14` · `Prisma` · `Pinecone` · `LangChain` · `Clerk` · `Supabase` — **[live demo →](https://academic-twin-rouge.vercel.app)**
